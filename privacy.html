Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how the game "Mousedoku - Where's the Mouse" (the "Game"), package name com.mnnss.mousecheese.game, handles your information when you use it on Android devices.

1. Information we collect

The Game does not collect any personal data. You do not need to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

2. Data stored only on your device

The Game stores some settings and your progress only on your device, such as your current level, stars, IQ score, language, and sound and vibration preferences. This data is never sent to any server, we cannot access it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. Permissions

  • Internet: used only to check whether a connection is available. The Game does not send any data about you through it.
  • Vibration: used for light haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in the settings.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and use your device advertising ID and technical device and app data (such as IP address, device type and your interaction with ads) to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and may show personalized ads depending on your consent. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom we ask for your consent through a consent form before ads are shown. You can manage your choices, reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). For more information see Google's advertising policies. To measure how well our TikTok ad campaigns perform, the Game also uses the TikTok Business SDK, which may send TikTok technical events such as installing and opening the Game and completing a level or the tutorial, together with the advertising ID, IP address and technical device and app data, in order to measure and improve ad results. We do not send TikTok your name, email address or phone number. You can opt out of personalized ads and reset your advertising ID in your device settings. For more information see TikTok's Privacy Policy. The Game does not use any other analytics tools.

5. Sharing of data

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not sell or share it with anyone.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including children.
